Every time a Sale is deducted straight from warehouse stock (beyond what was issued to a salesperson that day — the "Direct Sale" figure on the Inventory Reconciliation report), it's parked here so an Admin can confirm it isn't a duplicate deduction. Stock is not held back — it deducts immediately as before; this is a review/audit layer on top. Confirming or flagging a row here does not change any stock figures. Confirming a row with a salesperson also assigns it to a Stock Issue (automatically, when there's only one open that day — otherwise you'll be asked which one), so it's reconciled against the right one.
