Business Details
Used to decide CGST+SGST (same state as customer) vs IGST (different state) on every invoice.
Bank Details (shown on invoice for customer payments)
Invoice Numbering & Terms
Invoices auto-number as PREFIX/FY/0001, e.g. INV/2026-27/0001.
Note on e-invoicing: if your business's aggregate turnover has crossed the government's e-invoicing threshold, invoices also need a live IRN + QR code generated through the GST e-invoice portal — that requires a separate registered API/GSP connection and isn't something a locally generated PDF can produce. The invoices generated here cover all the standard statutory particulars (Rule 46) for businesses below that threshold, or as the printable/PDF copy alongside your IRN-registered process.
GST Filing Scheme
Governs the due dates shown on the GST Filing tab and used for reminders. QRMP's GSTR-3B due date (22nd or 24th) is worked out automatically from your State above.
GST Due-Date Reminders
In-app alerts on the Dashboard and GST Filing tab always show upcoming due dates — this switch only controls whether emails are also sent. Emails only go out while the app is running (python app.py), same as everything else in this app.
Email (SMTP) Settings (needed only if reminder emails are on)
For Gmail/most providers this needs to be an app-specific password, not your normal login password.
